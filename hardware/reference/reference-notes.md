# concept reference images

all three images are ai-generated concept sketches -- gemini-generated visualizations
of what inkblotter's rug tufting output and hardware could look like.
these are not photos of a real build or a real finished rug.
they exist to communicate the vision.

## reference-source-design.jpg

concept sketch of what the generative source image looks like as a flat digital design.
palette 3 composition: dark teal, cream, off-white, lavender, blush, black borders.
flat color regions, hard border lines, two regions with concentric ring detail.
this is what the engine would ingest -- the contract the generative source fulfills.

## reference-arm-in-progress.jpg

concept sketch of the robotic arm mid-execution on a vertical tufting frame.
shows the target hardware architecture: aluminum rail frame on easel legs, tufting gun
on a carriage, backing cloth stretched across the frame, partially complete rug building
up region by region. a second frame with a traced design is visible in the background.

useful as a visual brief for the hardware build -- this is the shape of the thing.

## reference-finished-rug.jpg

concept sketch of the finished tufted rug in a real living space context.
shows the target end product: consistent pile, clean region borders, the concentric
ring regions rendered in yarn, palette 3 in physical material form.
useful for communicating the commercial output -- what someone is actually buying.

## pipeline as concept

across the three images the full pipeline is visualized:
flat generative source -> arm executing on frame -> finished rug in space.
this is what we are building toward, not what exists yet.
